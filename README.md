**How I work**

- Retries must be safe: a payment or booking sent twice still happens once.
- Put invariants where they can't be bypassed: in the database, not only in the app.
- Say what isn't verified yet: tested locally, on staging, or in production.
- Write it down for the next engineer: ADRs, runbooks, dated risk registers.
